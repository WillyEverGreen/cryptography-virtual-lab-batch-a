3. MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION (RFC 2104)

HMAC(K, m) = H((K' ⊕ opad) || H((K' ⊕ ipad) || m))
Where ipad = 0x36 repeating 64 times, opad = 0x5c repeating 64 times.
