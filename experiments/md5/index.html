3. MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION (RFC 1321)

Round 1: F(X, Y, Z) = (X ∧ Y) ∨ (¬X ∧ Z)
Round 2: G(X, Y, Z) = (X ∧ Z) ∨ (Y ∧ ¬Z)
Round 3: H(X, Y, Z) = X ⊕ Y ⊕ Z
Round 4: I(X, Y, Z) = Y ⊕ (X ∨ ¬Z)
